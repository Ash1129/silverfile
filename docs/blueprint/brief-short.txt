SilverFile: Kushim, a financial firm, wants a multi-agent system that prepares a draft Form 1040-SR for senior clients, with a provenance record showing where every dollar came from, for a human preparer to review. It never files, never gives tax advice, and never states an amount it cannot source. An intake agent talks to the taxpayer one plain question at a time (resumable, no unneeded personal data, test SSNs only). Extraction reads W-2 images and 1099 forms in parallel. Eligibility checks 1040-SR eligibility, filing status and scope against a cited corpus. Computation fills every derived line through deterministic tax-math tools, never model arithmetic. Review adversarially checks the return and sends work back in a bounded loop. An orchestrator routes between them. Out-of-scope items, document conflicts, advice requests, a spouse who died in the year, and signs of elder abuse pause for a Kushim employee, who resolves them in a dashboard. LangGraph; six agents is a firm rule.
